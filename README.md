My project is a binomial expansion calculator based on the infinite series It is designed to approximate values for irrational numbers to a certain degree of accuracy dependent on how many terms you choose to have in your series. The expansion works better the larger the base is, as the convergence speed (The rate at which the series converges for n number of terms) is proportional to how close the base is to zero. Basically, the closer your base is to zero, the less accurate the approximation will be. The effect only becomes significant around when the base is lower than 1.5. I am currently working on a way to potentially counter this,probably by making my loop more efficient so I can generate more terms without an overflow error, and also adding in a way to measure your accuracy, which is the more important thing.
